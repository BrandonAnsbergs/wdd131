MISSION STATEMENT

BRIGHAM YOUNG UNIVERSITY-IDAHO

Brigham Young University-Idaho was founded and is suooirted and guided by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Its mission is to develop disciples of Jesus Christ who are leaders in their homes, the Church, and ther communities.

The university does this by:

  1. Building testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ and fostering its principles in a wholesome academic, cultural, and social environment.
  2. Providing a high-quality education that prepares students of divers interest and abilitites for lifelong learning and employment.
  3. Serving as many students as possible within resource constraints.
  4. Delivering education that is affordable for students and the Church.
